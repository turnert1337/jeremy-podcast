Setup · one-time tasks

S1 — Show identity (blocks everything else)

Before hosting or music can finish, you need three artifacts: a show name, a one-paragraph description, and cover art (3000×3000 JPG/PNG — required by Apple). The research report's positioning section is the raw material for the description. Decide the name with Jeremy on the next call — it's the one thing that's annoying to change later.

S3 — Hosting & distribution (how a podcast actually gets posted)

The mechanics, once, plainly: you never upload to Spotify or Apple directly. You upload the MP3 to a host; the host maintains an RSS feed (a machine-readable list of your episodes); Apple, Spotify, YouTube, and every other app just read that feed. Publish once at the host → it appears everywhere. Directories take 24 hours–10 days to approve a new show the first time, so submit before you need it.

HostCostWhy / why not
Spotify for Creators (was Anchor)Free, unlimitedThe zero-friction start. Auto-distributes to Spotify; submit the RSS to Apple/YouTube yourself.
RSS.com (free plan)Free, unlimited episodesOne-click submission to Apple + Spotify + Amazon; a more neutral home than Spotify's own host.

Recommendation: start on RSS.com free (one-click directory submissions, easy to migrate away from later — RSS feeds redirect). Move to Transistor when the n8n flow is ready to auto-publish. Setup order: create host account → upload cover art + description → submit to directories → paste the RSS link into the flow board.
