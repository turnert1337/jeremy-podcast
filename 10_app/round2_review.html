2 · The long-episode question — researched answer

The data: completion drops hard with length in general-audience shows — ~48% median completion past 60 min, and 90–120-min episodes see ~45% total drop-off. But conversation/panel formats are the exception that sustains 60–90+ (All-In runs ~90; Rogan and Acquired run 2–3+ hrs profitably) because the audience self-selects for long-form. The decisive factor isn't length — it's the first 2–3 minutes: 20–35% of listeners are lost in the first five regardless of runtime, and shows that open strong see ~40% higher completion. Your cold open is strong, and chapters in the show notes let skimmers jump.

My recommendation: ship episode 1 at ~97 min. Front-load (cold open → 7s sting → ≤25s intro, all inside the first 90 seconds), publish chapters, and treat completion-to-the-end as the wrong metric for a practitioner show — returning listeners is the right one. Aim future episodes at ~75 via the run-of-show clock, not by cutting this one deeper.
