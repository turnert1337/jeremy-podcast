## Doc-vs-doc discrepancies found

1. **AI Hero launch date (the big one).** 02a implies AI Hero itself launched/opened enrollment May 17, 2026, and uses that to explain the Feb–Oct 2026 YouTube inflection. 02b, with tweet citations, shows AI Hero already had a paid cohort course concept in March 2025, a first course section built by April 2025, 1,000 cohort signups by July 3, 2025, and a cohort start on July 14, 2025 that Pocock called his "most successful product launch ever" on July 15, 2025. **Resolution: 02b is correct about the product's origin (2025). 02a's May 17, 2026 date is real but belongs to a later, larger cohort ("AI Coding for Real Engineers," 2,500+ students) — not the product launch.** 02a should be corrected to: AI Hero launched in 2025; a record-sized cohort opened enrollment May 17, 2026 and ran June 1–12, 2026, coinciding with (not causing the start of) the YouTube inflection.
2. **Skills-repo star-spike dates.** Not actually a discrepancy — both are real, distinct events on re-check: 02a's April 27, 2026 spike (+5,551 stars, to 27,115 total, #1 trending Apr 26–29, confirmed via star-history.com and news.one-man-company.com) and 02b's September 4–6, 2026 spike (+2,200+/day, #2 trending, confirmed via mer.vin). Both happened; neither is the cause of the YouTube inflection (see #1 below).
3. **The inflection's actual timing vs. both proposed causes.** This is the sharpest finding of this pass. 02a's own YouTube data shows the inflection already underway between Feb 11, 2026 (152K) and Mar 29, 2026 (169K). But Wayback snapshots of aihero.dev at Feb 11 and Mar 1, 2026 show **no active cohort being promoted** — the next cohort didn't open enrollment until May 17, 2026 — and the skills repo's first viral spike was April 27, 2026. Both of 02a's cited causes happen *after* the inflection is already visible. The repo's creation date (2026-02-03) is the only candidate event that precedes the inflection, but a repo merely existing (pre-virality) is a weak mechanism for driving YouTube subscriber growth. **Neither doc has identified a cause that actually precedes the March 2026 acceleration — this should be flagged as an open question, not stated as resolved.**

## Safe-to-chart ruling

- **Chart as fact:** the full YouTube subscriber series 2022–2026 including the Feb–Oct 2026 inflection shape (independently spot-checked, 5/5 points matched exactly); the skills-repo current star count (283,910 vs. React's 250,819) and both of its confirmed spike events (Apr 27 2026, Sept 4–6 2026); the skills-repo creation date (2026-02-03); Total TypeScript's current pricing structure; the 2022 "3+ tips/week" cadence as a historical (not current) data point.

- **Do not draw a causal arrow.** Both proposed explanations for the March 2026 YouTube inflection (AI Hero cohort, skills-repo virality) postdate the inflection's own visible start — this is now a confirmed timing mismatch, not just an unproven claim.
